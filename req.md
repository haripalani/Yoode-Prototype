### 1. Premium Quality
* **Filename**: `quality.jpg`
* **Card Shape**: Tall Rectangle
* **Ideal Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (Square) or 4:5 (Portrait)
* **Content Ideas**: A beautiful close-up of premium fabric textures, intricate embroidery, a neatly folded high-end polo, or a quality assurance badge. 

### 2. Made to Your Specification
* **Filename**: `custom.jpg`
* **Card Shape**: Wide Rectangle (Image sits on the right half)
* **Ideal Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (Square) or 4:3 (Landscape)
* **Content Ideas**: A t-shirt surrounded by design swatches/tools, a screen showing the Yoode customization interface, or a side-by-side of a digital logo turning into a physical print.

### 3. Built for Bulk
* **Filename**: `bulk.jpg`
* **Card Shape**: Square (Image sits on the bottom half)
* **Ideal Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 3:2 (Landscape)
* **Content Ideas**: Huge towering stacks of colorful t-shirts, branded shipping boxes stacked neatly in a clean warehouse, or a massive grid of folded apparel.

### 4. Multiple Customization Options
* **Filename**: `printing.jpg`
* **Card Shape**: Square (Image sits on the bottom half)
* **Ideal Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 3:2 (Landscape)

### 5. Pan-India Reach

* **Ideal Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (Square) or 4:3 (Landscape)

### 6. Dedicated Support
* **Filename**: `support.jpg`
* **Card Shape**: Square (Image sits on the bottom half)
* **Ideal Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 3:2 (Landscape)
