The Process

How to Create Your Own Jersey

Four simple steps take your jersey from an idea to a finished kit, ready for your team.

01

Choose Your Style

Choose your preferred neck, sleeve and fit from options including polo, crew, V-neck, mandarin, raglan, half-sleeve and full-sleeve styles.

02

Share Your Idea

Share your logo, colours, artwork or reference on WhatsApp. No design yet? Our team can create one based on your requirements.

03

Approve The Mockup

Review your digital mock-up with names, numbers and sponsor placements. Request changes until every detail is ready for final approval.

04

Production & Delivery

Once approved, your jerseys are printed, stitched and quality-checked in-house, then delivered or collected from a Yoode Experience Centre.

Sponsor Logos
