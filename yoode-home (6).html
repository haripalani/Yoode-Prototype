DTG
01/ 07
Chapter 01 · Direct-to-garment · 180 GSM combed cotton

Ink that soaks in.

Water-based ink sprayed straight into the knit. It sinks between the loops instead of sitting on them, so a photograph prints with every gradient and the shirt still feels like a shirt.

  • Best forPhotos, gradients, art
  • Fabric100% cotton
  • Minimum1 piece
  • Wash life50+ washes
TeesSweatshirtsTotes
DTG
DTF
02/ 07
Chapter 02 · Direct-to-film · 320 GSM fleece

A print that sits on top.

The artwork is printed on film, pressed onto the fleece at heat, and the film peeled away cold. What stays is a bold, flat layer with white ink built in, so it works on black, on polyester, on anything.

  • Best forLogos and vectors
  • FabricAny, including poly
  • Minimum1 piece
  • Wash life60+ washes
HoodiesJerseysCapsBags
DTF
Embroidery
03/ 07
Chapter 03 · Embroidery · Pique cotton

Thread, four thousand times.

Your artwork is digitised into a stitch file, then a needle lays satin thread across the pique, pass after pass. It does not print; it becomes part of the cloth, and it outlasts the garment.

  • Best forLogos, monograms
  • FabricPique, twill, fleece
  • Minimum1 piece
  • LifeOutlasts the garment
PolosCapsJacketsTowels
Embroidery
Coloreel
04/ 07
Chapter 04 · Coloreel · Cotton twill

One thread, every colour.

A single white thread, dyed on the machine as it feeds. Gradients and photoreal logos in embroidery, with no thread changes and no colour limit.

  • Best forGradient logos
  • FabricAs embroidery
  • Minimum1 piece
  • ColoursUnlimited
CapsPolosJackets
Coloreel
Screen
05/ 07
Chapter 05 · Screen printing · Cotton jersey

Pull once, print five hundred.

Ink pulled through a mesh stencil, one colour per screen. Thick, matte, opaque, and once the screens are made every extra piece is cheap and fast. Built for events, uniforms and merch drops.

  • Best for1–6 solid colours
  • FabricCotton, blends
  • Minimum50 pieces
  • Wash life50+ washes
Event teesUniformsTotes
Screen
UV
06/ 07
Chapter 06 · UV printing · Brushed steel

Colour that cures on steel.

Droplets of ink land on the metal and an ultraviolet lamp sets them the instant they touch. Full colour on bottles, pens and boxes, slightly raised, glossy and scratch resistant.

  • Best forFull colour, hard goods
  • SurfaceMetal, glass, wood, plastic
  • Minimum1 piece
  • FinishGloss, raised
BottlesPensNotebooksPhone cases
UV
Engraving
07/ 07
Chapter 07 · Laser engraving · Full-grain leather

Burned in. Never off.

A laser traces your artwork into leather, wood or steel and takes the surface with it. No ink, nothing to fade or peel. The finish for gifts, awards and anything meant to be kept.

  • Best forGifts, awards
  • SurfaceSteel, wood, leather
  • Minimum1 piece
  • FinishPermanent
BottlesPensPlaquesGift boxes

Before you order

Answers to the usual questions.

Still have one? A real person replies within two hours on working days.

Ask us →
Do you offer bulk discounts?+
Yes. The per-piece price steps down with quantity, and screen printing gets cheaper the more you print. Tell us the number and we'll quote the tier the same day.
What is your typical turnaround time?+
Your free mockup lands within 24 hours. Most orders ship within 5 to 10 working days after you approve it. Single pieces at an experience centre can be ready the same day, and rush orders are possible when the stock is in.
Can I mix and match sizes and colours?+
Yes. One order can carry any mix of sizes and shades of the same product at the same price. Names and numbers per piece are fine too.
Which printing method is best for my design?+
Photos and gradients go DTG on cotton or UV on hard goods. Logos and flat-colour vectors go DTF for small runs and screen printing from 50 pieces. Anything that should feel premium and last goes to embroidery, Coloreel or engraving. Send the file and we'll recommend.
Do you have a minimum order quantity?+
No. One piece is an order. The only exception is screen printing, where the screens make sense from about 50 pieces.
How do I contact customer support?+
WhatsApp or call +91 8069 750 293, email info@yoode.com, or walk into any of the four experience centres. Replies within two hours on working days.
